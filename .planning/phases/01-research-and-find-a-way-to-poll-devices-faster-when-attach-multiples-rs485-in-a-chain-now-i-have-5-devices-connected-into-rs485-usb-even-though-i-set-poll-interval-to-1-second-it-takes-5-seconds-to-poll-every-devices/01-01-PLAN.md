<objective>
Optimize RS485 poll cycle speed by reducing read timeout (500ms → 150ms configurable), splitting inter-frame delay (100ms flat → 30ms reads / 100ms resets), and adding cycle duration monitoring.

Purpose: A 5-device cycle currently takes 3–5s due to conservative 500ms timeouts and 100ms IFD per device. These changes bring it to ~900ms, fitting within a 1s poll interval.

Output: Modified src/config.rs, src/poller.rs, src/main.rs, and config.toml with all D-01 through D-09 decisions implemented.
</objective>
